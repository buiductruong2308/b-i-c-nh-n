Kiến An - High School

Kien An High School, located in Hai Phong City, is one of the most prestigious public high schools in Northern Vietnam. With a history spanning over 60 years, the school has built a strong reputation for academic excellence, discipline, and well-rounded student development. The school consistently ranks among the top high schools in the city in terms of academic performance and university entrance results. Its dedicated team of experienced teachers fosters a learning environment that emphasizes both knowledge and character building. Students at Kien An High School are encouraged to think critically, pursue innovation, and actively engage in extracurricular activities. In addition to strong academic programs in mathematics, literature, foreign languages, and natural sciences, the school promotes cultural awareness, moral values, and civic responsibility. Many alumni have gone on to excel in fields such as education, engineering, medicine, and public service. Kien An High School continues to be a symbol of pride in Hai Phong, nurturing generations of students who are not only academically capable but also socially responsible and resilient in a fast-changing world.

HSB - Hanoi School of Business and Management

Hanoi School of Business and Management (HSB), a member of Vietnam National University, Hanoi, is a prestigious institution specializing in business, management, and interdisciplinary education. Established in 1995, HSB was one of the first units in Vietnam to offer MBA programs and continues to be a pioneer in leadership and innovation training. HSB offers a wide range of academic programs including Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral degrees. At the graduate level, key programs include the Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Non-Traditional Security Management (MNS), and Master of Technology and Entrepreneurship Management (MOTE). These programs are designed to prepare future leaders with strategic vision, innovation capacity, and practical problem-solving skills. In undergraduate education, HSB provides modern majors such as Business and Technology Management (MET), Marketing and Communication (MAC), Talent and Human Resource Management (HAT), and Management and Security (MAS). The curriculum is designed to integrate theory with real-world experience, delivered by both academic experts and industry professionals. With a strong international orientation and partnerships with universities in Australia, France, and Singapore, HSB aims to equip students with a global mindset, professional ethics, and entrepreneurial spirit to thrive in a fast-changing world.
